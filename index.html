Privacy Policy for Space Dodge

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Space Dodge (the “Game”) uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Space Dodge is made by RTF Gaming (“we”, “us”). It applies to the Android app on Google Play and to the same game when it is opened in a web browser.

Short version.

  • Your progress is saved on your device. RTF Gaming does not run its own servers for the Game and does not receive your game data.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google stores your leaderboard score and a backup of your save in your Google account. Your gamer tag and best score are shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Optional rewarded ads come from Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. For advertising, Unity receives your device’s advertising ID, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address) and how you interact with ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask before ads are personalised. Anyone can change their choice in Settings → Privacy → Privacy & ads choices; US players also have a “Do not sell or share” switch there.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The Game does not send usage reports or crash reports, and we do not sell your personal information for money.
  • Space Dodge is meant for players aged 13 and over.

1. Who we are

Space Dodge is developed and published by RTF Gaming. For privacy questions or requests, email tushanemclean@gmail.com.

2. Information saved on your device

The Game keeps this information in the app’s private storage (browser storage when played on the web):

  • Progress — best score, points earned and spent, unlocked and selected skins, daily reward streak and the last day you claimed it, and whether you finished the tutorial.
  • Settings — sound and music levels, controls, vibration, notifications and other options, plus which music tracks you heard recently, so tracks do not repeat too often.
  • Purchase records — for each lives pack you buy: the product, number of lives, time, the price and currency shown by Google Play, and the Google Play order reference.
  • Cached store prices — the last lives-pack prices received from Google Play, so the shop can show them offline.
  • Leaderboard cache — if you use Google Play Games, the last leaderboard loaded (up to 50 entries with public gamer tags and scores), your last rank, and your recent rank history.
  • Random install ID — an ID created by the Game on first launch. It is used only to merge your own saves and is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID.
  • Google Play Games sign-in state — your Play Games player ID and gamer tag, so the Game can show that you are signed in.
  • Your ad privacy choice — whether you allowed personalised ads, your “Do not sell or share” setting and when you chose. It stays on this device and is passed only to the ad software (see section 5).

You can copy this save to a file you choose with Settings → Export / Import. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

You can play without signing in. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the Game uses Google Play Games Services to:

  • Identify you in the Game — Google gives the Game your player ID and gamer tag.
  • Post your score — after a run, your score is sent to the Space Dodge leaderboard on Google Play Games. The leaderboard is public, so other players can see your gamer tag, score and rank.
  • Show live ranks — the Game loads the leaderboard from Google to show your rank, the rank chip during a run, and messages such as “Passed {name}” that use other players’ public gamer tags.
  • Back up your save — your save (best score, points, skins, settings, daily streak, purchase records, tutorial status and the random install ID) is stored with Google Play Games Saved Games in your Google account, so you can restore it on another device.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can change your profile visibility or delete your Play Games profile and its data in Play Games profile privacy & settings.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Lives packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The Game receives the purchase result from Google (such as the product, order reference and purchase status) and loads the current prices in your local currency. The Game saves a purchase record on your device as described above. Google’s handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Rewarded ads (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

Watching an ad is always optional. A completed ad gives you a revive in the current run. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) with Unity Ads. To have an ad ready, the ad software starts soon after the Game opens (after you make your choice below, if you are asked), even if you never watch an ad.

According to Unity’s policies, the ad software collects and shares with Unity, for advertising: your device’s advertising ID; your approximate location, which Unity works out from your IP address (the Game itself never reads GPS or precise location); app and ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, watched to the end or tapped; and device and app details such as model, operating system, language and app version. Unity uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if allowed, personalise ads. The Game uses the advertising ID only for advertising and tells Unity that it is not directed at children.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland — before any ad is loaded, the Game asks whether Unity and its ad partners may personalise ads. Accept and Decline are equally easy. If you decline, you still get ads and revives, but the ads are not personalised. If we cannot tell where you are, we ask anyway.
  • United States — Settings → Privacy has a Do not sell or share my personal information switch. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on, we tell Unity that you opted out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for targeted ads.
  • Everyone — Settings → Privacy → Privacy & ads choices shows your current choice and lets you change it at any time. Choosing Decline there also sends the do-not-sell signal.

Your choice is stored on your device and passed to Unity LevelPlay before the ad software starts. A change is passed on straight away; some ad partners only apply it the next time you open the Game.

To decide which question to show, the Game looks only at information already on your device: the country of your SIM card and mobile network, your language and region settings, and your time zone. This is not stored or sent anywhere, and the Game needs no location permission.

You can also delete or reset your advertising ID and manage ad personalisation in your Android settings (how to manage ad settings), or use the Data Privacy icon in a Unity ad. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules a simple reminder on your device (for example, that your ship is waiting and daily points are ready). They are created on your device and are not sent from our servers. You can turn them off in the Game’s settings or in Android settings.

7. Analytics, crash reports and other connections

  • This version of the Game does not send usage analytics or crash reports to us or anyone else.
  • We do not use Firebase or our own cloud servers for the Game.
  • On the web, the Game uses a service worker to cache its own files so it can run offline. It does not send your data anywhere.
  • Apart from the Google and Unity services above, the Game does not connect to other servers.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money or share it with data brokers. Information goes only to the services above: Google (Play Games, Play Billing) and Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads). For advertising, the ad software shares your advertising ID, approximate location and app and ad interactions with Unity and its ad partners. Under some US state laws this may count as a “sale” or “sharing” for targeted ads. You can opt out with Do not sell or share my personal information in Settings → Privacy, or by choosing Decline in Privacy & ads choices.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device — data stays until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings (Apps → Space Dodge → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the Game. On the web, clear the site data in your browser.
  • Google Play Games — your score and Saved Games backup stay in your Google account until you delete them. You can delete your Play Games profile and data in Google Play settings.
  • Purchases — Google keeps purchase history under its own policies.
  • Ads — Unity keeps ad data under its own policies. You can ask Unity to stop using and delete it with the Data Privacy icon in a Unity ad, or as described in Unity’s policy.

How to ask for deletion. Email tushanemclean@gmail.com with the subject “Space Dodge data deletion”. RTF Gaming holds no copy of your game data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side; we will confirm this and help you delete data on your device, in Google Play Games and with Unity, using the steps above. We reply within 30 days.

10. Children

Space Dodge is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the Game tells its ad provider that it is not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under the age at which you can consent to personalised ads where you live (16 in some countries), please choose Decline or ask a parent. If you believe a child has used the Game and want help removing their data, contact us.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, UK or some US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to withdraw consent, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted ads, and to object to some uses. Because we do not keep game data on our side, most requests are handled through the controls above or by Google and Unity. Contact us and we will help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security and international transfers

Data on your device is kept in the app’s private storage. Google and Unity use encrypted connections and may process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Changes

If the Game’s data use changes, we will update this policy and the dates at the top.
