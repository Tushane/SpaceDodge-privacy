Privacy Policy for Space Dodge

Effective date: 12 September 2026 · Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy describes how RTF Gaming (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile and web game Space Dodge (the “Game”). By installing or playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

Short version. Space Dodge stores progress on your device. If you use Google Play Games, Google may process your gamer profile so we can show ranks. If ads or in-app purchases are enabled in a store build, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Google Play process data as described by those services. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Space Dodge is developed and published by RTF Gaming. For privacy questions, contact: tushanemclean@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you play, the Game may process:

  • On-device game data — pilot name you choose, high score, points, lives, settings, purchase records stored locally, and a random device identifier used only to merge your own progress. This stays in your browser or app storage (for example localStorage).
  • Google Play Games profile (only if you sign in) — player ID and display name provided by Google so we can submit scores and show leaderboards.
  • Advertising data (store builds with LevelPlay) — advertising ID, approximate device and ad-performance data collected by Google so rewarded ads can be shown. We use this to offer an optional “watch ad to revive” reward.
  • Purchase data (if you buy life packs) — product ID and purchase status from Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Optional cloud sync — if a cloud API base URL is configured, score and profile events may be sent to that server so ranks can update across devices.

We do not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, precise GPS, photos, or microphone.

3. How we use information

  • Save and restore your game progress and settings.
  • Display local and, when available, Google Play or cloud leaderboards.
  • Deliver optional rewarded ads and apply the in-game reward only after a completed view.
  • Complete optional in-app purchases and restore what you bought on that device.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve gameplay (aggregated or on-device only unless a cloud API is set).

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — providing the Game you chose to install.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping scores fair, securing the Game, basic analytics.
  • Consent — personalized advertising and, where required, the advertising ID, via Google’s consent / UMP message when that is shown in a store build.

5. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in, leaderboards, scores. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) / Google Mobile Ads — rewarded ads. See Google Privacy Policy and LevelPlay help.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases processed by Google.
  • Hosting / cloud — only if we enable a Space Dodge cloud API for ranks.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share data with data brokers for money.

6. Advertising choices

Rewarded ads are optional. You can keep playing without watching ads (using lives, daily rewards, or purchases where offered). On Android you can reset or opt out of ads personalization in device settings (Google → Ads). In the EEA/UK a consent form may appear before personalized ads.

7. Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear site/app storage or uninstall. Google Play Games scores remain on Google’s services under Google’s retention rules. Cloud rank entries, if enabled, are kept while the service runs and may be deleted on request where we control the server.

8. Security

Progress is stored on-device. Cloud or Play features use HTTPS and Google’s authentication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children

Space Dodge is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will delete what we control.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data we control, or object to certain processing. You can also:

  • Clear the Game’s local storage or uninstall the app to remove on-device data.
  • Sign out of Google Play Games in device or Play Games settings.
  • Use advertising opt-outs described above.

EEA/UK users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Google and any cloud host we use may process data in the United States or other countries. Those providers apply their own safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses where required).

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
